# Demo script (3 minutes)

Product on screen within 20 seconds. Every step happens live on Arbitrum.

| Time | Screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Home page | "Ana runs a market stall in Liberdade. Her consórcio has ten members and one organizer holding everyone's cash. Last year an organizer in her street disappeared with the pot." |
| 0:20 | Live feed and counters | "Roda is the same circle with no organizer. Every line here is a real transaction on Arbitrum." |
| 0:35 | Create a circle: 3 seats, 2-minute rounds | "A circle is a contract. Nobody, including us, can move its money." |
| 0:55 | Three phones join; seats fill on the wheel | "Each member locks a deposit. The circle starts when the last seat fills." |
| 1:15 | Two members pay, one does not; one bids a discount | "Need the pot now? Bid a discount. The discount goes to the members who wait." |
| 1:40 | Settle: payout sound, winner's seat lights up | "The round closes. The missed payment came out of that member's deposit, so the pot is whole." |
| 2:00 | Arbiscan transaction, then the completed circle's history | "Here is the settlement onchain. This circle ran all three rounds; each member's record is now their reputation." |
| 2:25 | Contract code: no owner, invariant test | "The hard part is the accounting: an invariant test checks the contract always holds exactly what it owes." |
| 2:45 | What is live vs. next | "Live today on Sepolia. Next: join from a link with a passkey, and Robinhood Chain." |
